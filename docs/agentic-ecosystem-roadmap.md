# Somnora Agentic Ecosystem Roadmap

This sequence records the agreed path from the hackathon Workbench to a broader Somnora ecosystem. Build each step as a focused, verifiable slice. Do not imply that later integrations are live before they are implemented.

## Product loop

Signals lead to observations. Observations may become invitations. Invitations wait for consent. Consented actions can move across devices. Outcomes return as inspectable context. Only user-approved learning becomes durable memory.

## Sequence

1. **Universal Context Timeline**: completed in the guided Workbench on August 28, 2026. Dream, Daily, Eureka, sleep context, activities, Nora reasoning, and user-confirmed growth now share one source-linked chronology. The event contract includes future Fitness and Nutrition domains without presenting those adapters as active.
2. **Generalized Nora Action Runtime**: completed on August 28, 2026. Four typed action contracts now share explicit single-action consent, route validation, bounded progress, guarded lifecycle transitions, expiry, cancellation, and privacy-safe outcomes. The seeded transport runs the complete contract. The live version 1 relay remains intentionally limited to Three Beautiful Things until later device adapters are built.
3. **Nora Action Desk**: completed on August 28, 2026. A new primary Workbench destination now separates noticed context, proposals, approvals, active handoffs, completions, failures, declines, and stopped actions. Every record exposes authority, consent, route, progress, source, privacy, and memory boundaries. Current state is reducer-derived and older records are clearly labeled privacy-safe seeded history.
4. **Consent and Autonomy Console**: completed on August 28, 2026. Dream, Daily, Eureka, Sleep, Activity, Fitness, and Nutrition now share explicit Off, Observe, Suggest, and Prepare boundaries. Consequential action authority remains locked to Ask every time. The current Eureka workflow enforces both suggestion and preparation boundaries, policy preferences persist as compact enums, Action Desk remains the readable activity history, and disconnected Fitness and Nutrition adapters cannot be elevated.
5. **Growth Without Gamification**: completed on August 28, 2026. Growth is now a primary source-linked destination with then and now comparisons for a user-confirmed boundary, returned curiosity, and agency across declined and completed activities. Users can confirm, defer, or qualify each story for the current session. Reviews return to the universal timeline but do not become durable memory. The view contains no points, ranking, disclosure rewards, or penalties for quiet periods.
6. **Activity Studio**: completed on August 28, 2026. A new primary destination organizes Discover, Connect, Create, Reflect, and Reset ideas, then adapts their variants to time, energy, movement, social bandwidth, seeded weather, and privacy. Browsing never starts an activity. Six Line Story and Private Release are interactive here, Three Beautiful Things returns to the consent-based Home flow, Breathing Reset is labeled existing Somnora continuity, and unfinished ideas have no start control.
7. **External World Connectors**: completed on August 28, 2026. Context Sources now exposes source, freshness, permission, failure, reason, and retention boundaries for location, weather, calendar availability, events, Fitness, and Nutrition. Current weather uses one-time browser location only after a click, calendar files are reduced locally to availability, maps open only after a user action, and local events remain explicitly fictitious demo fixtures. Fitness and Nutrition remain disconnected. Normalized weather and open time can adapt Activity Studio without granting execution authority.

## Ecosystem principle

Somnora should feel like one Nora with specialized capability adapters, not several disconnected assistants. Watch sends minimal timely signals to iPhone. iPhone remains the trusted identity and consent bridge. The Workbench provides the broadest view for reflection, inspection, and planning.

## Standing boundaries

- No diagnosis, treatment claims, or replacement of professional care.
- No hidden autonomous messaging, scheduling, purchasing, or sharing.
- No live data claim without a verified source and visible disclosure.
- No private text in logs, analytics, screenshots, or notification previews by default.
- User corrections and forgetting controls must remain first-class product actions.
